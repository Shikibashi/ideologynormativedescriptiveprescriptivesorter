# Section 3: workbench and validation

The research workbench continues to derive targets dynamically. The selected target now also exposes its curated candidate shelf, qualitative anchor profile, neighbor discriminants, false-positive audits, and source metadata. Draft authoring remains separate from the curated shelf. Structural validation is available for candidates, while the promotion gate remains fail-closed.

No cognitive-review field is added. The workbench’s text describes the missing evidence plainly and does not imply that a candidate is ready for respondent deployment.
