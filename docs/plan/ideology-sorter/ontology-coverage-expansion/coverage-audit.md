## Interpretation

The audit proves that the research surface and direct authoring coverage are expanding, not that the sorter is scientifically validated. The 204 production prompts still measure declared shared facets, and eleven existing anchors now have explicit target-tagged production coverage: Right-Libertarianism, the six earlier continuation branches, and the four current canonical branches—Ecosocialism, Left-Libertarianism, Libertarian Socialism, and National Conservatism. The 1,428 research candidates improve branch and context coverage while remaining outside the respondent flow. All 119 ontology and registry targets have a source-backed three-layer starter block; the current target measurement states are 91 catalog-only, 5 scored-indirect, 12 registry-only, and 11 dedicated-scored. The fascist, National Socialist, Neo-Fascist, white-nationalist, and related high-risk branches include analytical boundary items with explicit false-positive holds; they do not activate those fields or assign a normative ranking. Direct target tags close an authoring/traceability gap, not empirical measurement validity. The result contract adds a combined pattern only after all three production layers are covered; it is an equal-layer display composition, not an empirical or normative ranking.

## Required next coverage waves

1. Review the completion tranche for source fit, mechanism-level wording, neighbor distinctness, false-positive boundaries, and jurisdictional or temporal limits; treat the records as research material, not ready-made measures.

3. Reassess production measurement only after the required authority, substantive review, and later empirical evidence gates are available. No cognitive review or substitute evidence is part of this cycle.
4. Re-run this audit after every future change; candidate coverage is a prerequisite for review, not a promotion decision.
